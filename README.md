# Silver Spoon

A VCMI mod for players who want their heroes spoon-fed.

## Submods

- **Level Scaling** - each hero gains +20% experience gain per level (from level 1), and for every level after the first: +1% double-damage chance, ignore 1% of enemy defense, +2% spell damage, +100 gold per day, +10% land movement and +100 sea movement points (multiplied by hero level minus one). All four primary skills also get +25%, so each hero gains most in its strongest stats. Rough terrain also costs 50 fewer movement points per tile, like Advanced Pathfinding. Heroes whose specialty already gives one of these bonuses keep only their own version of it.
- **Unlimited Secondary Skills** - removes the 8-skill limit.
- **Supremacy** - replaces Fire, Air, Water and Earth Magic with one skill, Supremacy, that gives all four schools at its level. Heroes who started with an elemental skill start with Supremacy (Adrienne at Expert), heroes are no longer offered the elemental skills, and universities, Witch Huts and Scholars stop teaching them; the Conflux Magic University sells Supremacy instead. Icon: original artwork made for this mod.

## Requirements

VCMI 1.7.0 or newer.
